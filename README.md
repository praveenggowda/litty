# Litty 💡

An AI agent that helps your real experience shine.

Every job is different, but most of us send the same CV everywhere. Litty changes that.

Give Litty a job description or a job link. It understands the role, gets to know your experience, and helps you create a CV tailored to that opportunity, without changing your story.

Just you, at your best.

## What Litty knows about you

* Your experience, work projects and personal projects
* Your skills and technology stack
* Your achievements, qualifications and certifications
* Your STAR stories and production incidents
* Your career preferences

## How it works

1. Share a job description or job URL.
2. See how well you fit the role.
3. Review and approve Litty’s suggestions.
4. Get your tailored CV.
5. Want a cover letter? Just ask.

## The promise

* Only your real experience.
* No exaggeration.
* Every claim traces back to your profile.
* Nothing changes without your approval.

## Why Litty?

Inspired by the word *lit*: to brighten things up.

Litty brings your experience to light and helps you present it at its best.

## Getting started

You need Python 3.14 or later.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/praveenggowda/litty.git
cd litty
python3 -m venv .venv
source .venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
python main.py
```

On Windows, activate the virtual environment with `.venv\Scripts\activate` instead.
